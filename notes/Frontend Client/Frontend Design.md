These are some of the keys to replicating the success of the MTGA / Hearthstone clients.
### **1. Smooth, Responsive UX**

- **Fast animations** – Snappy card movement, clear transitions.
- **Low input latency** – Instant response to clicks and drags.
- **Fluid drag-and-drop** – Cards should feel tactile and satisfying.

### **2. Clear Game State Representation**

- **Obvious visual hierarchy** – Important elements stand out (e.g., active cards glow).
- **Minimal clutter** – Keep the board clean and easy to read.
- **Contextual UI hints** – Show tooltips and highlights when needed.

### **3. Strong Audio-Visual Feedback**

- **SFX for every action** – Dragging, hovering, playing cards should all feel distinct.
- **Subtle animations** – Cards should "breathe" slightly, hover effects should feel alive.
- **Impactful effects** – Big plays should have big visual/audio cues.

### **4. Intuitive Controls**

- **Click, drag, and tap should all feel natural** (especially for mobile).
- **Undo support** – If possible, let players cancel moves before finalizing.
- **Keyboard shortcuts (for PC)** – Speed up play for advanced users.

### **5. Performance Optimization**

- **Async loading** – Load assets smoothly to avoid lag.
- **Keep FPS high** – Prioritize smooth animations over visual excess.
- **Minimal UI latency** – Input should never feel sluggish.

### **6. Social & Quality-of-Life Features**

- **Fast reconnection** – If a player disconnects, they should recover smoothly.
- **Spectator mode** – Helps with community engagement.

### **7. Player Interaction**
- **Opt-in text Chat** – Can feel less lonely, we have a good community so far. I don't think there will be so much hate. In any case, it's opt-in. 
- **Emotes** - You can still spam these at your opponent, they are forced to see them
