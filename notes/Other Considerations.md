## Hand Smoothing
*Probably not necessary*

In MTGA there's what's called [hand smoothing](https://mtgazone.com/mtg-arenas-opening-hand-algorithm-and-smoothing/).

>The system draws an opening hand from each of two separately randomized copies of the decks, and leans towards giving the player the hand with the mix of spells and lands (without regard for color) closest to average for that deck.

For the ADC, Is it better to tweak opening hands behind the scenes or to leave it up to rng entirely?

Ultimately this is an MTG issue Algomancy should have solved with the recycle-for-mana system. But maybe there's an analogous issue in Algomancy that could be remedied with some similar algorithmic weighing of the scales?
